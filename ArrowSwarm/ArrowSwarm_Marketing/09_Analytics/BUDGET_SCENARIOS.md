# Growth budgets — no spend performed

All amounts below are **illustrative caps**, not CPI forecasts, vendor quotes or promised outcomes. USD; exchange rate/tax/account minimums not included. User must separately approve the actual platform, targeting and cap before any campaign.

| Scenario | Illustrative cap | Gate | Action |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Current default | Original organic work, voluntary playtests, manual reports and free tools within their verified limits. |
| Small learning test | $30 total:3days×$10 | Safe release, consent/store links, understandable revised creative, approved tracking and explicit spend approval | One platform/market/language/objective. This budget may not support a reliable CPI/D7/LTV conclusion. |
| Controlled expansion | $150 total:5days×$30 | Mature cohorts, no critical technical issue, written acceptable cost/risk and renewed cap approval | Repeat the clearest creative; stop and review at the cap. No automatic scaling. |

## Decision rules

Stop immediately for broken store/consent/attribution, a crash/ANR cluster, wrong audience/market, misleading creative, reward failure or cap risk. Resolve technical issues before scaling.

Use real platform spend/click/install exports, keeping organic and paid cohorts separate. Suggested planning floors of1000comparable impressions per creative or30valid installs are only reminders about small samples, not statistical significance guarantees. Below sufficient evidence, label the test inconclusive.

There is no measured netLTV, CPI or retention target today. Do not invent profitableCAC/ROAS or an industry benchmark. CPM=spend/impressions×1000; CPC=spend/clicks; CPI=spend/attributed_installs, all with fixed attribution windows and nonzero known denominators.

Never click own live ads or generate test traffic against production ad units. Official test ads and internal QA are excluded from business results.
