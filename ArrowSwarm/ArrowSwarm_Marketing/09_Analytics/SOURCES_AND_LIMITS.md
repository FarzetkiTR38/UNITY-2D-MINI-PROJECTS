# Sources and limits

Accessed13November2026. No real-player or campaign measurements were supplied. Definitions below are research/planning, not live instrumentation, legal certification or performance claims.

- [YouTube Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en): raw starts/replays and engaged views differ.
- [Instagram Best Practices](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/): account-specific dashboard guidance, not reach guarantees.
- [TikTok creator tips](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/scroll-stopping-tips-from-top-tiktok-creators?lang=en-GB): hook/caption suggestions, not measured ArrowSwarm performance.
- [Android vitals](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9844486?hl=en): user-perceived crash/ANR definitions. Current page shows overall1.09%crash and0.47%ANR bad-behavior thresholds; these are neither ArrowSwarm results nor acceptable product targets. Recheck at launch.
- [Play Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en) and [User Data](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en): include third-party SDK behavior.
- [AdMob UMP](https://developers.google.com/admob/unity/privacy): consent/privacy choices and request gating.
- [Unity Analytics privacy](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/analytics/privacy-and-consent/manage-data-privacy): separate privacy responsibility; no assumption that the SDK is installed here.
- [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng/): Art5/6 purpose/minimization/lawful-basis principles; not legal advice.
- [KVKK mobile recommendations](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7751/Mobil-Uygulamalarda-Mahremiyetin-Korunmasina-Yonelik-Tavsiyeler): search text read, full-page retry timeout; full-text final review remains.
