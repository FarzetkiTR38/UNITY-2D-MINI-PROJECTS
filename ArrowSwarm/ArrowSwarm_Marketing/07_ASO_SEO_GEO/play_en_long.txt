Every arrow has a direction. The next move is yours.

ArrowSwarm combines directional arrow puzzles with three different ways to read a board, plan a route and clear a path.

SHAPES
Find the order hidden inside a picture made of bent arrow paths. Study the penguin, heart and other silhouettes, choose an arrow with a clear exit, and work through the shape one move at a time. The current Shapes campaign contains 50 puzzles.

COLOR SHIFT
An open path is only part of the answer. An arrow must also meet a wall of the same color. Rotate the colored outer frame in quarter turns while the arrows stay in place. Each level gives you a limited rotation budget. The current Color Shift campaign contains 50 puzzles.

DEFENSE
Bring puzzle decisions into a moving defense board. Open routes for arrows and send them toward enemies traveling around the board. Plan your shots as the action unfolds.

READ THE ROUTE
In the puzzle modes, a blocked shot returns and costs a life. Clear the arrows while protecting your three lives. Use the board's paths and the direction of each arrow to decide what can leave next.

FOLLOW YOUR PROGRESS
Shapes and Color Shift keep their own campaign progress. Earn up to three stars for a completed puzzle, based on the lives you have left. Light and dark themes give the boards different looks.

Start with a shape, learn the exits and find your next move.
