# R2 Turkish narration

Three updated original sentences (clips 00, 05, 06) were synthesized with tr-TR-AhmetNeural at +8% using edge-tts. Five unchanged clips (01, 02, 03, 04, 07) are byte-for-byte copies of the prior pilot; their hashes are retained. Only the three public creative sentences were sent to the speech service; no Instagram audio, account cookies, credentials or private files were used.

Narration is editorial, not Unity AudioListener output. Each raw MP3 and conservatively trimmed 48kHz mono PCM WAV is retained. Timing is deliberately unscheduled so the editor can show A exiting alone, pause, and then introduce the remaining arrows. No claim of exclusive rights or cleared hosted-service terms for public release is made.

Tool source: https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts
