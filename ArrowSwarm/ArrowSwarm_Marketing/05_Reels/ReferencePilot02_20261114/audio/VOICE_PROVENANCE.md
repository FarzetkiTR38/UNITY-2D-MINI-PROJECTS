# Turkish pilot narration

Eight original Turkish sentences were synthesized with tr-TR-AhmetNeural using edge-tts. Only this public creative script was sent to the speech service. No reference-video audio, credentials, account cookies or private files were used.

The narration is added in editing and is not Unity AudioListener output. Raw MP3 and conservatively edge-trimmed 48kHz mono PCM WAV are retained with durations and hashes. There is no claim of exclusive rights or cleared third-party voice-service terms for a public release.

Tool source: https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts
