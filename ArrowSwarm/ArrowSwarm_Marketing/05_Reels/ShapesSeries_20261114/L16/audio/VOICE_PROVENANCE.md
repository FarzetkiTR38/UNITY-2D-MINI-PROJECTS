# Shapes 16: Turkish editorial narration

The ördek episode has 42 arrows, as verified from its authored level. Its opening and congratulations were synthesized from original Turkish text using tr-TR-AhmetNeural at +8%. The four unchanged question/answer/resume lines reuse the approved R2 pilot WAVs byte for byte. One direction-neutral explanation was generated once for all six episodes and copied unchanged.

Only the short public script was sent to the speech service; no reference audio, private player data, credentials or cookies were sent. Narration is editorial, not native Unity AudioListener capture. Raw MP3 and conservatively trimmed 48kHz mono PCM WAV are retained with duration/hash/no-clipping checks. The previously approved shared Play Store promo narration is not modified.

Technical checks do not claim subjective listening or exclusive voice rights.
