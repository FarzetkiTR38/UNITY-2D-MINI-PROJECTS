# Creative Short01 narration

Three original public Turkish sentences were synthesized with tr-TR-AhmetNeural at+8% using edge-tts. Only those public creative sentences were sent; no private data, credentials or Instagram audio were used. Raw MP3s and conservatively trimmed48kHz monoPCM16 WAVs are retained.

Clip03 is a byte-for-byte copy of the approved, corrected-P SharedOutro01/cta_voice.wav. It was not regenerated, and no unedited base MP3 is misrepresented as the source of that composite WAV. The store destination remains mock. All narration is editorial, not Unity AudioListener output. Technical PCM/hash checks are not a subjective listening certification. Parent aligns final timings.
