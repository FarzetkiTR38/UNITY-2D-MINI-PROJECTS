# Defense Pilot01 Turkish narration

Six original public Turkish sentences were synthesized with tr-TR-AhmetNeural at +8% using edge-tts. Only those six creative sentences were sent to the speech service. No Instagram audio, account cookies, credentials, player state or private files were used. Raw MP3s and conservatively edge-trimmed 48kHz mono PCM16 WAVs are retained.

Clip06 is a byte-for-byte copy of the approved corrected-P SharedOutro01/cta_voice.wav, SHA256 3cffc7f86966e7eb7c4c6e7b0524ea7a2ed59c9c013ad2305cfe0adc857e4c23. It was not regenerated. The copied cta_source_provenance.json records its composite onset patch; the unedited base MP3 alone is not misrepresented as the source of that corrected WAV. The store destination remains mock.

All narration is editorial audio, not Unity AudioListener output. Measured clip durations and requested 17-second-cut windows are in voice_QA.json. PCM and hash checks are technical checks, not a subjective listening certification. No claim of exclusive rights or cleared hosted-service terms for public release is made.
