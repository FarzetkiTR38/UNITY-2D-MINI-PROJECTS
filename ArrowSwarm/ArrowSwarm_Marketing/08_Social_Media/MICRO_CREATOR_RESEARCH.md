## Qualification and outcome

For this campaign, use **1,000–100,000 followers/subscribers on the relevant channel** as a working micro-creator band. This is a planning definition, not an industry standard. A qualified entry needs all three: a current count visibly supplied by its own channel, dated recent Android/mobile puzzle or indie-game coverage, and a public business contact route whose scope is clear.
